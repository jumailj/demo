A first launch pad

A place to experiment.

Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centres (IEDCs) give students a platform to experiment, collaborate and develop inventive ideas into prototypes of viable products and services.

LEAD IEDC is part of Kerala Startup Mission’s initiative to build an innovation culture in higher education. It helps students take their first steps into entrepreneurship with access to new technology, capable mentors, early risk capital and wider exposure.

  1. Try the idea

    Question, explore and experiment.

  2. Build with others

  3. Make a prototype

    Turn a concept into something testable.

  4. Take it further

    Use feedback to find the next step.
