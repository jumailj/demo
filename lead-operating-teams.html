The LOT system

A leadership laboratory on campus.

LEAD Operating Teams (LOTs) are student-led functional units responsible for campus activities and institutional initiatives. Each LOT is guided by a faculty in-charge, bringing structure, support and accountability to the work.

From events, communication and operations to innovation and social outreach, students gain hands-on experience in planning, coordination and leadership. Every decision has weight; every outcome is owned.

  1. Student-led

    Students take an active role in running campus functions and initiatives.

  2. Faculty-guided

    A faculty in-charge supports each unit with oversight and guidance.

  3. Learning by doing

    Planning, teamwork and accountability develop through real work.
