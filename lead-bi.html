Embracing entrepreneurship

From job seeker to job creator.

LEAD is nurturing the next generation of young entrepreneurs through entrepreneurial development support alongside its MBA programme. The aim is to help young people discover and develop their entrepreneurial talent — and to become job creators as well as capable professionals.

LEAD brings together the culture, competencies and support aspiring founders need to explore a business idea while pursuing their education. Students can learn management and put that learning to work on a venture of their own.

A distinctive business-school model

A B-School mindset. An E-School spirit.

LEAD brings entrepreneurial development into the student experience, creating an environment where aspiring founders can explore a venture while studying. Students can connect business learning with the real questions of building and running an enterprise.

Governments, institutions and the business community are increasingly supporting entrepreneurship. LEAD-BI aims to give aspiring entrepreneurs a place to begin, learn and develop with support around them.

The way forward

Study management. Build what matters.

LEAD-BI brings incubation, mentoring, modelling and funding support together under one roof. Students can pursue a recognised university MBA while working toward the dream of becoming an entrepreneur during their time at LEAD.

If you are already building a business or are ready to explore your first idea, LEAD invites you to take the next step with a community committed to entrepreneurial development.
