Eligibility

Who can apply.

Minimum requirements for the MBA are listed below. Confirm MCA eligibility with the admissions team before you apply.

MBA

  • A bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST).
  • A valid score from CAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT or any other AICTE-approved management entrance test.

Admissions FAQs

Common questions

How long is the MBA and is it residential?

It is a 2-year (4-semester), full-time, residential programme at the LEAD campus in Dhoni, Palakkad. The residential model supports peer learning, industry interaction and continuous academic support.

Does LEAD offer an entrepreneurship option?

Yes. The Entrepreneurship MBA is a pathway within the MBA, supported by LEAD-BI and LEAD IEDC.

What facilities are available on campus?

The green, residential campus includes a Learning Resource Centre, advanced computing labs, hostels, sports facilities, a cafeteria and medical facilities.

What makes LEAD different?

A diverse peer group, academicians, industry experts and student-run initiatives, with experiential learning through real-world projects and mentorship from Guinness World Record holder Dr. Thomas George K.

What is the placement record?

A 95%+ placement record, a ₹21.64 LPA highest package, ₹8.5 LPA median CTC and 500+ recruiters.
