Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What programmes does LEAD offer?

LEAD offers MBA and MCA programmes. The MBA also offers an entrepreneurship pathway supported by LEAD-BI and LEAD IEDC, alongside 10+ specialisations including Digital Marketing, Finance and HR.

What are the eligibility criteria for the MBA?

A bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST). Candidates must also have a valid score from CAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT, or any other AICTE-approved management entrance test.

How long is the MBA and is it residential?

It is a 2-year (4-semester), full-time, residential programme at the LEAD campus in Dhoni, Palakkad, Kerala.

What is the placement record?

LEAD has a dedicated Placement Cell and a 95%+ placement record, including a ₹21.64 LPA highest package, ₹8.5 LPA median CTC and 500+ recruiters.

Are scholarships or fee concessions available?

Yes. LEAD offers merit scholarships, government fee concessions (SC/ST/OBC) and education loan support through partner banks.

How can I contact the admissions team?
