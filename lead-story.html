Our story

A journey of shaping leaders with purpose.

Every milestone reflects the determination of our students, the dedication of our faculty and the trust of our community.

  1. 2010

    A vision becomes a campus

    LEAD College was established in Palakkad with an initial community of 120 students and a clear mission: develop leaders with courage, clarity and conscience.

  2. 2011

    Faith through the first storm

    When unexpected regulatory challenges tested the young institution, 58 students chose to stay. LEAD stood by them with free education, food and accommodation—a defining expression of its commitment to students.

  3. 2012

    The first leaders step forward

    The first 58 students graduated, turning perseverance into possibility. By the end of the year, the student community had grown to more than 360.

  4. 2015–20

    Milestones of trust and excellence

    A period of academic growth and recognition, including AICTE approval, NAAC and NBA accreditation, ISO 21001:2018 certification, GSAAA recognition and autonomous status under the University of Calicut.

  5. 2023

    Innovation expands: MCA begins

    LEAD introduced its Master of Computer Applications programme, bringing technology, business understanding and innovation together for a changing world.

  6. Today

    A greener future, a stronger legacy

    A plastic-free campus, solar energy initiatives, modern facilities and a learner-first approach—creating opportunities for the next generation of leaders.
