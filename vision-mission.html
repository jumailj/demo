How learning is designed

Learning with outcomes in view.

At LEAD, education is focused not only on what students learn, but on what they can achieve with that learning. Our Outcome-Based Education framework connects programme design with the knowledge, skills, attitudes and professional competencies needed in a changing business and technology environment.

  • Think critically
  • Lead responsibly
  • Innovate confidently
  • Contribute meaningfully

Programme objectives & outcomes

Capabilities for what comes next.

The frameworks below set out the capabilities our programmes aim to develop. Programme-specific documents remain the formal reference for approved outcomes.

MBA · Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  1. Management & entrepreneurship

    Apply management and entrepreneurial knowledge to develop innovative business solutions and create organisational value.

  2. Leadership & decision-making

    Demonstrate ethical leadership, analytical thinking and problem-solving to make informed, responsible decisions.

  3. Professional competence

    Use communication, teamwork, interpersonal skills and adaptability in dynamic professional environments.

  4. Ethics & social responsibility

    Uphold constitutional, ethical and humanistic values while acting responsibly in personal and professional life.

MBA · Programme Outcomes (POs)

  1. Management knowledge

    Apply management theories, concepts and practices to identify and solve complex business problems.

  2. Analytical thinking

    Use critical analysis to interpret information and make evidence-based business decisions.

  3. Value-based leadership

    Demonstrate ethical, value-based leadership in professional and organisational environments.

  4. Global business awareness

    Understand global, economic, legal and ethical aspects of business and their implications for organisations.

  5. Teamwork & organisational leadership

    Lead oneself and others towards shared goals and contribute effectively to diverse teams.

Programme-Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

MBA: Develop strategic management, entrepreneurial, analytical and leadership capabilities to address real-world business challenges.

MCA: Develop advanced computing, software development, analytical and technological problem-solving capabilities for contemporary industry and society.
