Right to Information Act, 2005

Right to Information.

Citizens can seek information about the activities of LEAD College by sending a written request to our Public Information Officer.

Information request procedure

How to make a request.

  1. Submit a written request with your name, address, contact telephone or mobile number and the information you are seeking.
  2. The reason for seeking the information need not be given.
  3. Address the duly signed request to the Public Information Officer, along with the required fee of ₹10.
  4. Copies of documents are charged at the rates fixed by the State Information Commission, Kerala.
