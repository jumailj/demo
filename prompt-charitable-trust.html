Responsibilities

Governance with clear roles.

Trust members are not directly involved in the day-to-day functioning of the institute. They have delegated powers to the Governing Body, while retaining authority over matters relating to the Trust’s charitable activities for society.

Meetings

Twice each year.

Meetings of Prompt Charitable Trust are convened once every six months at the direction of the Chair. Additional meetings may be convened as required under the Chair’s instructions.
