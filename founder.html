The story

From enterprise to education.

In 1994, Dr. Thomas began his professional journey as an entrepreneur, founding Prompt Computers, a start-up in Palakkad focused on the sales and service of computers and accessories. Prompt Computers went on to develop India’s largest touch-screen network for Indian Railways.

In 2002, he turned his attention to training, working with young people and students on the skills that help them grow. His work with JCI took him to more than 35 countries as an international trainer.

He is Chief Mentor of Turning Point, LEAD’s intensive seven-day life-skills programme. Over more than 290 sessions, he has guided participants through reflection, challenge and purposeful action.

LEAD College grew from his commitment to education that develops the whole person. Today he continues to mentor students and build an institution focused on human excellence.

Author & creative voice

The left brain speaks to Thomman’s right brain.

Preneur is co-authored by two sides of one person: Thomas George, grounded in practicality, and Thomman, guided by creativity. Together, they offer practical advice and original strategies for people pursuing entrepreneurial success.

The book makes the ideas behind the brain’s hemispheres approachable, exploring how readers can develop their potential and take deliberate steps on an entrepreneurial journey.
