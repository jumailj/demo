Academic governance

Academic Council & Boards of Studies

Academic direction and curriculum oversight for MBA and MCA.

Academic Council17 members

Formed after LEAD attained autonomous status, the Academic Council supports quality assurance and a relevant, coherent and innovative curriculum. It is the college’s primary academic governing body, overseeing academic standards, policies and practices. Its composition includes University representatives, external academic and industry experts, an alumni representative, MBA and MCA faculty, the Controller of Examinations, the Director and Deputy Director.

Termnominated members serve three years.Meetingsonce every six months.
  • Functions: scrutinise and approve Board of Studies proposals on courses, regulations, curricula, syllabi, instruction and evaluation; return proposals for reconsideration or reject them with reasons where needed.
  • Regulate admissions to programmes in line with Government policy.
  • Recommend new programmes, scholarships, studentships, fellowships, prizes and medals to the Governing Body, and frame award regulations.
  • Advise the Governing Body on academic matters and perform other functions assigned to it.

Institutional committees

Financial oversight and coordinated institutional leadership.

Finance Committee6 members

The Finance Committee oversees institutional finances, reviews budgets and expenditure, and advises the Governing Body on responsible use of funds. Its composition includes the Director, an administrator nominated by the Governing Body, the Finance Officer and the Officer for Finance and Accounts.

Termthree years.Meetingsonce every six months.
  • Functions: prepare annual budgets and estimates; monitor income, expenditure and resource use; review audited statements and financial performance; recommend programme fees; and review development proposals.

Governing Body15 members

Constituted by Prompt Charitable Trust, the Governing Body is the institution’s apex governing body. It connects the college leadership with the Trust’s objectives, oversees operations, mobilises resources and takes strategic decisions. It may add, amend or repeal provisions of the constitution in accordance with the rights defined in it.

Termnominated members serve five years.
  • Responsibilities: review institutional performance and financial statements; decide on strategic plans, HR policies and appointments; approve the Trust’s agenda concerning the institution; and consider matters submitted by management.

Programme quality

Programme Advisory Committee

Regular review supports relevant outcomes and continuous improvement.

Programme Advisory Committee10 members

The Programme Advisory Committee (PAC) supports quality and continuous improvement of the MBA programme. It reviews Programme Outcome attainment using academic performance, alumni and industry feedback, and other evidence, then recommends academic and administrative improvements.

Termnominated members serve three years; student representatives serve one year.Meetingsat least once every six months.
  • Responsibilities: evaluate Programme Outcome attainment; review curriculum delivery, teaching methods and learning resources; assess results, placements and stakeholder feedback; recommend improvements to course structure, teaching and assessment; and align programme outcomes with accreditation and outcome-based education requirements.
